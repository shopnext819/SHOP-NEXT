Help & Support for SHOP NEXT

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. How do I log in to my account?

For security and ease of use, SHOP NEXT uses Google Authentication exclusively. Traditional usernames and passwords are not supported or required! Follow these steps:

  • Step 1: Go to the main homepage of SHOP NEXT.
  • Step 2: Click on the "Login with Google" button from the Sidebar or Top Menu.
  • Step 3: A secure Google pop-up will appear. Select your preferred Gmail account.
  • Step 4: Once verified by Google, you will be automatically logged in to your dashboard!

2. How do I contact a seller?

If you have questions about a product or an existing order, you can communicate directly with the shop owner using our Inbox messaging system:

  • Step 1: Navigate to the specific product page you are interested in or go to your Orders list.
  • Step 2: Look for the Seller's Profile section on the page.
  • Step 3: Click the "Message Seller" or "Contact" button.
  • Step 4: Type your query in the Inbox and hit send. The seller will be notified instantly!

3. What is your return policy?

We want to ensure your complete satisfaction. Therefore, we provide a robust 7-Day Return Policy based on specific conditions:

  • Eligibility: Returns are accepted only if the product is physically defective, damaged upon arrival, or significantly not as described by the seller.
  • Condition: The item must remain completely unused and enclosed in its original, undamaged packaging.
  • Process: Go to your Orders list, select the item in question, and click "Request Return". Upload photo evidence of the defect to speed up the process.
  • Timeline: Once the seller and administration approve the return, the refund will be processed and returned to your source account within 3 to 5 business days.

4. How do I become a seller on SHOP NEXT (Step-by-Step Guide)?

Becoming a seller is easy! Follow these exact steps to register your store on our platform:

  1. Navigate to Sidebar: First, open the main sidebar menu.
  2. Get Started: Click on the "Get Started" button located in the seller section of the sidebar.
  3. Create New Account: Select "Create New Account" to begin your seller application.

Once the registration form opens, you will need to fill it out carefully. Here is exactly what you need to provide:

Phase 1: Initial Store Setup

  • Store Name: Enter the name of your shop (e.g., Electro Zone).
  • Store Code: Enter a unique identifier for your store (e.g., EZ-001).
  • Store Description: Write a brief description of what you are selling.
  • Upload Store Banner: Upload a banner image that represents your store.
  • Upload Profile Picture: Upload your store's main logo or profile picture.
  • Click "Start Application ❯" to proceed.

Phase 2: Step 1 - Contact & Location

  • Primary Business Category: Select the category of goods you sell (e.g., Smartphones, Computers, Gaming).
  • Complete Business Address: Provide your full warehouse or shop area.
  • City & Postal Code: Enter your city and postal/zip code.
  • Email & Phone: Enter your official business email address and active mobile number.

Phase 3: Step 2 - ID Card Verification (KYC)

  • Full Legal Name: Enter your exact name as printed on your National Identity Card.
  • Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth.
  • ID Card Pictures: Upload clear images of your ID Card Front and ID Card Back.

Phase 4: Step 3 - Final Details & Submit

  • Social Media Links: Enter the URLs for your store's Facebook Page and Instagram Profile.
  • Terms and Conditions: Read carefully and check the box to agree to the terms.
  • Click "Submit ✔" to finish your application. Our team will review and approve your account shortly!

5. How is my shipping address added?

To speed up your checkout process and assure accurate delivery tracking, we feature an advanced Auto-fill Location tool integrated with OpenStreetMap:

  • Step 1: During checkout or profile creation, look for the "Auto-Fill Location" button.
  • Step 2: When clicked, your browser will ask for location permission. Click "Allow".
  • Step 3: Our system will immediately ping OpenStreetMap and retrieve your exact Coordinates, City, and Postal Code.
  • Step 4: The form fields will automatically populate so you don't have to type them manually!

6. How do I know if a product is in stock?

Inventory management on SHOP NEXT is highly transparent. You can explicitly see stock levels on every individual product page:

  • Availability Tag: A green "Available" tag signifies the item is currently active and can be purchased immediately.
  • Stock Counter: Sellers dynamically manage their stock quantities. You will see a live counter (e.g., "15 Items Left" or "Out of Stock") so you never place a false order.
  • Reservations: As soon as you complete checkout, the server automatically updates and deducts the inventory.
